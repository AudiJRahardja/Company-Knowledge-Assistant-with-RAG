# Brightnest Rewards Loyalty Program

## Joining
Brightnest Rewards is free. Sign up in the Brightnest app, on brightnest.co.id, or at any store with your phone
number.

## Earning points
- Earn **1 point for every Rp10,000** spent, online and in store.
- Points are added within 24 hours after delivery or purchase.
- Points are not earned on shipping fees, gift cards, or Brightnest Care+.

## Using points
- **100 points = Rp10,000** off your next purchase.
- Points can be combined with one promo voucher.
- Points expire **12 months** after they were earned.

## Member tiers
| Tier | Yearly spend | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | Under Rp5,000,000 | Points, birthday voucher Rp50,000 |
| Gold | Rp5,000,000 or more | 1.5x points, birthday voucher Rp100,000, free same-day delivery |

Tiers are reviewed every year on 1 January based on the previous year's spending.

## Returns and points
If you return an item, the points earned on it are removed. If you paid with points, they are returned to your
account.
