# Products and Services

## Product categories
- **Cookware**: frying pans, woks, pots, steamers, baking trays. Our Brightnest Kitchen range includes
  non-stick, stainless steel and cast iron pans. Prices from Rp89,000 to Rp2,500,000.
- **Small kitchen appliances**: rice cookers, air fryers, blenders, kettles, stand mixers and coffee makers.
- **Tableware**: plates, bowls, glassware, cutlery and serving sets.
- **Kitchen tools**: knives, cutting boards, spatulas, measuring tools.
- **Home storage**: food containers, shelving, boxes and organisers (Nest Living).
- **Bedding and bath**: sheets, pillows, towels and bath mats (Nest Living).
- **Furniture**: dining tables, chairs, shelves and small cabinets, available online and in the BSD showroom.
- **Home decor**: lamps, vases, frames and plants.

## Services
- **Cookware trade-in**: bring any old pan to our Kemang, Dago or Darmo store and get a Rp50,000 voucher per
  pan (maximum 3 per visit) toward a new Brightnest Kitchen pan. Old pans are recycled.
- **Free knife sharpening**: Saturdays at the Kemang store, for knives bought at Brightnest.
- **Furniture assembly**: Rp150,000 per item, booked at checkout.
- **Gift wrapping**: Rp15,000 per item, online and in store.
- **Wedding and gift registry**: create a registry in the app and share it with your guests.
- **Business orders**: special pricing for offices, hotels and restaurants. Email sales@brightnest.co.id.

## Product information
Every product page lists materials, size, care instructions and warranty. Our product team tests Brightnest
Kitchen cookware on gas and induction stoves; check the product page to see which stoves a pan works on.
