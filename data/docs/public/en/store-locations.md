# Store Locations and Opening Hours

All times are local time (WIB).

## Jakarta: Kemang (flagship)
- Address: Jl. Kemang Raya No. 21, South Jakarta
- Opening hours: Monday to Sunday, 10:00–22:00
- Services: click & collect, cookware trade-in, free knife sharpening on Saturdays

## Jakarta: Kelapa Gading
- Address: Jl. Boulevard Raya Blok M3 No. 5, Kelapa Gading, North Jakarta
- Opening hours: Monday to Sunday, 10:00–22:00
- Services: click & collect

## Tangerang: BSD
- Address: Jl. BSD Grand Boulevard No. 12, Tangerang
- Opening hours: Monday to Sunday, 10:00–21:00
- Services: click & collect, furniture showroom

## Bandung: Dago (our first store)
- Address: Jl. Ir. H. Juanda No. 140, Bandung
- Opening hours: Monday to Saturday, 09:00–21:00; Sunday, 10:00–20:00
- Services: click & collect, cookware trade-in

## Bandung: Pasteur Outlet
- Address: Jl. Dr. Djunjunan No. 77, Pasteur, Bandung
- Opening hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 09:00–18:00; **closed on Mondays**
- Outlet store selling end-of-line and display items. Outlet items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

## Surabaya: Darmo
- Address: Jl. Raya Darmo No. 45, Surabaya
- Opening hours: Monday to Sunday, 10:00–21:00
- Services: click & collect, cookware trade-in

## Holiday opening hours
- All stores are **closed on the first day of Idul Fitri**.
- On the second day of Idul Fitri, stores open 13:00–21:00.
- On 24 and 31 December, all stores close at 18:00.
- On other public holidays, stores keep their normal opening hours.

## Click & collect
Orders placed online with click & collect are usually ready within 3 hours during opening hours. We hold
collected orders for 7 days.
