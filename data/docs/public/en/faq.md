# Frequently Asked Questions

## Payment
**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB), bank transfer (virtual account), QRIS, major e-wallets, and
cash on delivery (COD). In stores we also accept cash.

**Is cash on delivery available everywhere?**
COD is available in Jakarta, Tangerang, Bekasi, Bandung and Surabaya for orders up to Rp2,000,000.

**Can I pay in instalments?**
Yes. 0% instalments for 3, 6 or 12 months are available with selected credit cards on orders of Rp1,000,000
or more.

## Orders
**Can I change or cancel my order?**
You can cancel or change an order within **1 hour** of payment in My Orders. After that, the order may
already be packed; you can return it after delivery under our return policy.

**Do online prices match store prices?**
Usually yes, but some promotions are online-only or store-only. We do not price-match other retailers.

**Can I use more than one voucher?**
Only one promo voucher can be used per order. Brightnest Rewards points can be combined with a voucher.

## Account and app
**I forgot my password.** Use "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link is sent to your email.

**How do I delete my account?** Contact customer service or use Settings > Delete account in the app.

## Products
**Are your pans induction compatible?** Many are. Check "Stove type" on the product page.

**Do you sell spare parts?** Yes, for Brightnest Kitchen appliances (for example blender jars and air fryer
baskets). Ask customer service.

## Other
**Do you have a physical store near me?** See "Store Locations and Opening Hours" for our six stores.

**Do you ship internationally?** No, we currently deliver only within Indonesia.
