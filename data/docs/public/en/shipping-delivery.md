# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we deliver
We deliver to all of Indonesia. Some remote areas may take longer or have extra fees, which are shown at
checkout.

## Delivery options and times
- **Standard delivery**: 2–4 business days within Java; 4–8 business days outside Java.
- **Same-day delivery**: available in Jakarta, Tangerang, Bandung and Surabaya for orders paid **before 12:00
  WIB** on business days. Fee: Rp35,000.
- **Click & collect**: free. Ready within 3 hours at the store you choose.
- **Large furniture**: delivered by our own team within 5–10 business days. Optional assembly costs Rp150,000
  per item.

## Shipping fees
- **Free standard delivery** on orders of **Rp500,000 or more** within Java.
- Free standard delivery on orders of **Rp750,000 or more** outside Java.
- Below these amounts, the fee depends on weight and destination and is shown at checkout.

## Order processing
Orders paid before 15:00 WIB on a business day are usually packed the same day. Orders are not shipped on
Sundays or national public holidays.

## Tracking your order
You will receive a tracking link by email and in the app once your order is shipped. You can also ask our
customer service for your order status with your order number (format **BN-2026-XXXXXX**) and the email
address used for the order.

## Problems with delivery
If your parcel is late by more than 3 business days beyond the estimate, or arrives damaged, contact customer
service. Do not accept a parcel with clearly broken packaging; ask the courier to note the damage.
