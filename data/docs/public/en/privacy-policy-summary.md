# Privacy Policy Summary

This is a short summary. The full privacy policy is available at brightnest.co.id/privacy. We process
personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022).

## What we collect
- Account details: name, email, phone number, delivery addresses
- Order and payment history (we do not store full card numbers)
- Brightnest Rewards activity
- App and website usage data, with your consent for marketing cookies

## Why we use it
- To process and deliver your orders, returns and warranty claims
- To provide customer service
- To run Brightnest Rewards
- To send marketing messages, **only if you opt in**
- To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations

## Sharing
We share data only with partners who help us serve you, such as couriers and payment providers, and only as
needed. **We do not sell your personal data.**

## How long we keep it
Order and transaction records are kept for 10 years for tax and legal reasons. Other account data is deleted
within 30 days after you delete your account.

## Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent for marketing, at any time. Email
privacy@brightnest.co.id. We respond within 14 days.
