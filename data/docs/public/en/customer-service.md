# Contact Us and Customer Service Hours

## How to reach us
- **Live chat**: on brightnest.co.id and in the Brightnest app
- **WhatsApp**: +62 811-0000-1234 (messages only, no calls)
- **Phone**: 1500-123
- **Email**: help@brightnest.co.id (we reply within 1 business day)
- **In store**: any Brightnest store can help with orders, returns and warranty claims

For business and bulk orders (offices, hotels, restaurants), email sales@brightnest.co.id.
For media enquiries, email press@brightnest.co.id.

## Customer service hours (WIB)
- Monday to Friday: 08:00–20:00
- Saturday and Sunday: 09:00–17:00

Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next time we open.

## Customer service holiday schedule
Customer service is **closed** on:
- 1 January (New Year's Day)
- The first and second days of Idul Fitri
- 25 December (Christmas Day)

On all **other national public holidays**, customer service runs reduced hours, **09:00–15:00 WIB**, by
WhatsApp and email only (no phone or live chat).

Store opening hours on holidays are different from customer service hours. See "Store Locations and Opening
Hours".

The head office does not serve walk-in customers. Please visit a store instead.
