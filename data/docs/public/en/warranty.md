# Warranty Policy

## Warranty periods
| Product type | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Small kitchen appliances (blenders, rice cookers, air fryers, kettles, mixers) | 1 year |
| Brightnest Kitchen non-stick cookware (coating) | 2 years |
| Brightnest Kitchen stainless steel cookware | 10 years |
| Brightnest Kitchen knives | 5 years |
| Furniture (frame and structure) | 2 years |
| Other brands | As stated by the manufacturer |

The warranty starts on the date of purchase or delivery.

## What the warranty covers
Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship under normal household use. For example: an appliance that
stops working, a non-stick coating that peels under normal use, or a broken furniture joint.

## What the warranty does not cover
- Normal wear and tear, scratches and discolouration
- Damage from misuse, dropping, or not following the care instructions (for example, using metal utensils on
  non-stick pans or putting wooden items in the dishwasher)
- Commercial use (restaurants, catering)
- Repairs by anyone other than Brightnest or an authorised service centre

## How to make a warranty claim
1. Have your proof of purchase ready (receipt, order number, or Brightnest Rewards purchase history).
2. Bring the item to any Brightnest store, or contact customer service with photos or a short video of the
   problem.
3. We will repair or replace the item. If neither is possible, we will offer store credit for the purchase
   price.
Most claims are decided within 7 business days.

## Brightnest Care+ extended warranty
For small appliances, you can buy Brightnest Care+ at checkout. It adds **1 extra year** of warranty and costs
**10% of the item price**. Care+ must be bought together with the item.
