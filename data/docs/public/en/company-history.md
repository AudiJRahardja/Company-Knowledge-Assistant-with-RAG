# Our History

## 2012: A small shop in Bandung
Brightnest started in 2012 as a small kitchenware shop on Jl. Ir. H. Juanda (Dago), Bandung. Founders
Rina Hartono and David Kusuma were frustrated that good, affordable cookware was hard to find, so they began
importing and testing pans themselves.

## 2016: Going online
In 2016 we launched brightnest.co.id and started shipping across Java. Online orders quickly grew to half of
our sales.

## 2018: Our first Jakarta store
We opened our flagship store in Kemang, South Jakarta, in 2018, and moved our head office to Jakarta the same
year. We also launched our own cookware brand, Brightnest Kitchen.

## 2020: Nest Living and nationwide delivery
In 2020 we introduced Nest Living, our storage, bedding and decor brand, and expanded delivery to all of
Indonesia.

## 2021–2023: Surabaya and more stores
We opened our Surabaya store in 2021, our Kelapa Gading store in Jakarta in 2022, and the Bandung Pasteur
outlet in 2023.

## 2024–today
In 2024 we launched the Brightnest app and the Brightnest Rewards loyalty program. In 2025 we opened our second
Jakarta-area store in BSD, Tangerang, bringing us to six stores. Today we serve more than one million
customers a year.
