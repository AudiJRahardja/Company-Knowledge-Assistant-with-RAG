# About Brightnest

## Who we are
Brightnest Home & Living (PT Brightnest Home Indonesia) is an Indonesian home and kitchen retailer. We sell
cookware, small kitchen appliances, tableware, home storage, bedding and bath products, small furniture and
home decor. Customers can shop at brightnest.co.id, through the Brightnest mobile app, or at one of our six
stores in Jakarta, Bandung and Surabaya.

Our head office is at Jl. Wijaya Raya No. 88, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta. We have around 850 team members
across our stores, warehouses and head office.

## Our mission
Our mission is to make everyday home life a little brighter by offering well-made, practical products at fair
prices, backed by honest service.

## Our values
- **Practical quality**: we test products in real Indonesian kitchens and homes before we sell them.
- **Fair prices**: no fake discounts. A sale price is always compared with a price we actually charged.
- **Honest service**: we explain our policies clearly and keep our promises.
- **Care for the planet**: we reduce packaging and run a cookware trade-in program.

## Our house brands
- **Brightnest Kitchen**: cookware, knives and kitchen tools.
- **Nest Living**: storage, bedding, bath and home decor.

We also sell selected international and local brands.
