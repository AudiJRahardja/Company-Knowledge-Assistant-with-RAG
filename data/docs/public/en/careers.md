# Careers at Brightnest

## Working with us
We are a team of about 850 people in our stores, warehouses and head office in South Jakarta. We hire for:
- **Stores**: sales associates, cashiers, visual merchandisers, store managers
- **Warehouse and delivery**: warehouse staff, drivers, furniture assemblers
- **Head office**: e-commerce, marketing, buying, customer service, finance, HR and IT

Open positions are listed at brightnest.co.id/careers.

## How to apply
1. Apply online at brightnest.co.id/careers with your CV. We do not accept applications by WhatsApp.
2. Our recruitment team reviews applications within about 2 weeks. Shortlisted candidates are contacted by
   email or phone.
3. Interviews: usually one screening call and one or two interviews with the team.
4. Offer and background check.

The whole process usually takes 3–5 weeks.

## Internships
We offer 3–6 month internships for university students in marketing, e-commerce, data, design and IT. Intakes
open in January and July. Interns receive a monthly allowance.

## What we offer
Competitive pay, health insurance, an employee discount, training and clear career paths. Many of our store
managers started as sales associates.

## Beware of recruitment scams
Brightnest **never** asks candidates to pay any fee, buy training materials, or transfer money during
recruitment. Our recruiters only email from addresses ending in @brightnest.co.id. If you are unsure, email
careers@brightnest.co.id.
