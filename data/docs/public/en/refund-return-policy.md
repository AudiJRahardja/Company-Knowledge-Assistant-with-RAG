# Refund and Return Policy

## Change of mind returns
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery or purchase** if they are unused and in their original
packaging with all tags and accessories.

**Electrical appliances** (for example blenders, rice cookers, kettles and air fryers) can be returned for a
change of mind within **14 days**, and only if the box is unopened.

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, is faulty, or is not what you ordered, contact us within **30 days** of delivery.
We will offer a replacement or a full refund, and we cover the return shipping. Please keep the packaging and
send a photo of the problem to help@brightnest.co.id or WhatsApp +62 811-0000-1234.

Faults that appear after 30 days are handled under our warranty. See "Warranty Policy".

## Items that cannot be returned
- Items marked **"Final Sale"**, including most Pasteur Outlet items
- Personalised or engraved items
- Pillows, mattresses and mattress protectors that have been unsealed (for hygiene reasons)
- Gift cards and e-vouchers

## How to return
- **In store**: bring the item and your receipt or order number to any Brightnest store. In-store returns
  are free.
- **Online**: go to My Orders > Request Return in the app or on the website. Change-of-mind returns sent by
  courier have a **Rp25,000** return shipping fee, which is deducted from your refund.

## When you get your money back
- **Store credit**: issued immediately after we check the item.
- **Refund to your original payment method**: within **7–14 business days** after we receive and check the
  item. Bank transfer refunds can take up to 14 business days. Cash on delivery (COD) orders are refunded by
  bank transfer.
- Original shipping fees are refunded only for faulty, damaged or wrong items.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within 30 days, in store or online, subject to stock.

Our staff cannot offer exceptions to this policy beyond what is written here.
