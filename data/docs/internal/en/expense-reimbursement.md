# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

This policy is about **employees** getting money back for work expenses. (It is not the customer refund
policy.)

## What can be reimbursed
- **Business travel**: transport (economy flights, trains, taxis/ride-hailing for work trips) and hotels up to
  Rp900,000 per night (Rp1,200,000 in Jakarta and Bali).
- **Daily allowance (per diem)** on overnight work trips: **Rp250,000 per day within Java** and **Rp350,000 per
  day outside Java**. Meals are covered by the per diem; do not claim them separately.
- **Client entertainment**: with prior approval from your department head.
- **Work supplies and training**: if approved in advance by your manager.
- **Local transport**: ride-hailing for work errands during the day, and to get home after 21:00 when working
  late.

## What is not reimbursed
Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, upgrades to business class, family members' costs, and
expenses without a receipt (except per diem).

## How to claim
1. Go to **BrightHub > Expenses > New Claim**.
2. Add each expense with a photo of the receipt.
3. Submit within **30 days** of the expense. Late claims are rejected unless your department head approves.

## Approval and payment
- Your manager approves claims up to **Rp5,000,000**. Claims above Rp5,000,000 also need your **department
  head's** approval.
- Approved claims are paid **with your next salary** if approved before the 15th of the month; otherwise in
  the following month's salary.

## Cash advances
For trips longer than 3 days, you can request a cash advance in BrightHub at least 5 working days before the
trip. Settle it with receipts within 7 days after you return.
