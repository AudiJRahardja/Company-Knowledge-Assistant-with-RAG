# Employee Public Holiday Calendar 2026

This calendar is for **employees**. It follows the government's joint ministerial decree (SKB) on national
holidays and collective leave (cuti bersama) for 2026. (Customer service and store opening hours on holidays
are different; see the public pages.)

## National public holidays 2026
| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| 1 January (Thu) | New Year's Day |
| 16 January (Fri) | Isra Mi'raj |
| 17 February (Tue) | Chinese New Year (Imlek) |
| 19 March (Thu) | Nyepi (Day of Silence) |
| 20–21 March (Fri–Sat) | Idul Fitri 1447 H |
| 3 April (Fri) | Good Friday |
| 1 May (Fri) | Labour Day |
| 14 May (Thu) | Ascension of Jesus Christ |
| 27 May (Wed) | Idul Adha |
| 31 May (Sun) | Vesak Day |
| 1 June (Mon) | Pancasila Day |
| 16 June (Tue) | Islamic New Year |
| 17 August (Mon) | Independence Day |
| 25 August (Tue) | Prophet Muhammad's Birthday |
| 25 December (Fri) | Christmas Day |

## Collective leave (cuti bersama) 2026
- 18 March (Wed), 23–24 March (Mon–Tue): around Idul Fitri
- 24 December (Thu): Christmas Eve

At Brightnest, collective leave days are **extra paid days off and are not deducted from your annual leave**.

## Store and warehouse staff
Stores stay open on most holidays (see public store hours). If you work on a national holiday or a collective
leave day, you can choose either **holiday pay (2x your hourly rate)** or **one replacement day off** to be
taken within 30 days. Record your choice in BrightHub > Attendance.
