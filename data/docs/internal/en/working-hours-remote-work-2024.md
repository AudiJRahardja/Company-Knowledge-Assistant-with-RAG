# Working Hours and Remote Work Policy (2024)

## Office working hours
Head office staff work 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, **09:00–17:00** with a 1-hour lunch break.
Core hours, when everyone must be available, are **09:00–16:00**.

## Remote work
- Head office staff can work from home **1 day per week**, agreed with their manager.
- Employees on probation must work fully in the office.
- Remote work is not available for store and warehouse staff.

## Internet allowance
There is no internet allowance for remote work.

## Store and warehouse staff
Store and warehouse staff work shifts set by their store manager, 40 hours per week.
