# Annual Leave Policy

## How many days you get
- All permanent and contract employees get **12 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- You can start using annual leave **after passing probation** (3 months). In your first year, leave is
  prorated: 1 day for each full month worked in that year.
- **Long service bonus**: +1 day after 3 years of service, +2 days after 5 years, +3 days after 8 years
  (maximum 15 days per year).

Your current balance is shown in BrightHub > Leave.

## Carrying over leave
You can carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**;
after that they expire. Unused leave is not paid out, except when you leave the company (unused days of the
current year are then paid out).

## Booking leave
- Request leave in BrightHub (see "How to Request Time Off").
- For 1 day, request at least **2 working days** ahead. For 2 or more days, request at least **5 working days**
  ahead.
- Half-day leave is allowed (morning or afternoon).

## Peak periods for store and warehouse staff
Store and warehouse staff cannot normally take annual leave during:
- The **2 weeks before Idul Fitri**
- **15–31 December**
Exceptions need approval from your area manager.

## Collective leave
Government collective leave days (cuti bersama) are not deducted from your annual leave. See the Employee
Public Holiday Calendar.
