# Code of Conduct

## Respect at work
We treat customers and colleagues with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination based on religion,
ethnicity, gender, age, disability or any other personal characteristic are not tolerated, in person or online.

## Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager and HR if you have a personal interest that could affect your work, for example a family
member working at a supplier, or a side business that sells similar products. Declare it in BrightHub >
Declarations.

## Gifts and hospitality
- You may accept small gifts from suppliers or business partners worth up to **Rp500,000** per gift.
- Gifts above Rp500,000 must be declared to your manager and are usually returned or donated.
- **Never** accept cash, vouchers, or gifts during a tender or negotiation.

## Confidential information
Do not share internal information (sales figures, supplier prices, employee data, unreleased products or
internal policies) outside the company. This applies on social media too.

## Social media
You may say you work at Brightnest, but do not speak on behalf of the company. Media requests go to the
communications team (press@brightnest.co.id).

## Customer data
Only access customer data when you need it for your job. Never take customer data home or share it on
personal devices or chats.

## Speaking up
Report suspected misconduct, fraud or safety issues to your manager, HR, or the confidential ethics line:
**ethics@brightnest.co.id** or +62 811-0000-7777 (anonymous reports are accepted). Retaliation against anyone
who reports in good faith is a serious breach of this code.

## Breaches
Breaking this code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination, in line with Indonesian
labour law.
