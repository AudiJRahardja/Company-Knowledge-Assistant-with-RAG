# Employee Benefits

## Government social security
All employees are registered in **BPJS Kesehatan** (national health insurance) and **BPJS Ketenagakerjaan**
(work accident, death, old-age savings and pension). Brightnest pays the employer contribution; your part is
deducted from salary as required by law.

## Private health insurance
From your first day, you are covered by our group health insurance with **Asuransi Sehat Bersama**:
- **Inpatient**: covered up to the plan limit, room class depending on your grade.
- **Outpatient**: up to **Rp10,000,000 per year** (doctor visits, medicines, lab tests).
- **Dental**: up to **Rp2,000,000 per year**.
- **Glasses**: up to **Rp1,500,000 every 2 years** (frames and lenses).
- Your spouse and up to 3 children can be covered at no extra cost. Register them in BrightHub within 30 days
  of joining, marriage or birth.

Use your insurance card at partner hospitals and clinics (cashless), or claim back in the insurer's app within
30 days.

## Religious holiday allowance (THR)
**1 month's salary**, paid at least 7 days before Idul Fitri (or before your own religious holiday if you
choose). If you have worked less than 12 months, THR is prorated.

## Employee discount
- **25% off** at Brightnest stores and brightnest.co.id, using your employee ID.
- Not combinable with other promotions. Maximum **Rp10,000,000** of discounted purchases per year.
- The discount is for you and your household only. Reselling is not allowed.

## Learning budget
**Rp3,000,000 per year** for courses, books, or certifications related to your work, approved by your manager.
Claim it through BrightHub > Expenses.

## Wellness
- Free gym partnership at selected gyms near head office and stores.
- Free, confidential counselling: up to 6 sessions per year through our employee assistance program.
