# Sick Leave Policy

## Paid sick leave
Sick leave is paid at your full salary and is **not deducted from annual leave**.

## Telling your manager
If you are sick, tell your manager **before 09:00** (office staff) or **before your shift starts** (store
and warehouse staff), by phone or chat. Then record the sick day in BrightHub > Leave > Sick Leave within
**2 working days** after you return.

## When you need a doctor's note
- **1–2 consecutive days**: no doctor's note needed.
- **3 or more consecutive days**: a doctor's note (surat keterangan dokter) is required. Upload it in BrightHub.
- You can take up to **6 days per year without a doctor's note** in total. After that, every sick day needs a
  note.

## Long illness
If you are sick for more than 14 consecutive days, HR will contact you to discuss support and next steps.
Pay during long illness follows Indonesian labour law. Your private health insurance covers medical costs
(see Benefits).

## Caring for a sick family member
Sick leave is for your own illness. To care for a sick spouse, child or parent, you can take up to **3 days
of family care leave** per year (paid, with a doctor's note for the family member), or use annual leave.

## Work-related injuries
Report any injury at work to your manager immediately. Work accidents are covered by BPJS Ketenagakerjaan.
