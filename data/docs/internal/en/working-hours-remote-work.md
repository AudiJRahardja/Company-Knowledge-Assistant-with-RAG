# Working Hours and Remote Work Policy

This version is effective from **1 January 2026** and replaces the 2024 policy.

## Office working hours (head office)
- 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
- **Flexible start**: start any time between **07:30 and 10:00** and work 8 hours plus lunch.
- **Core hours**: **10:00–15:00**. Be available for meetings during core hours.

## Hybrid and remote work (head office)
- Head office staff can work remotely up to **2 days per week**, agreed with their manager.
- **Wednesday is the team anchor day**: everyone works from the office on Wednesdays.
- Employees on probation can work remotely 1 day per week, from their second month.
- Working from outside Indonesia needs written approval from HR and your department head, for at most
  2 weeks per year.
- When working remotely, you must be reachable on chat during core hours and use the company VPN.

## Internet allowance
Head office staff on a hybrid schedule receive an internet allowance of **Rp200,000 per month**, paid with
salary. No receipts needed.

## Store and warehouse staff (shift work)
- 5 shifts per week, **8 hours each including a 1-hour break**.
- Typical store shifts: **morning 09:00–17:00** and **evening 14:00–22:00** (depending on store opening hours).
- Shift schedules are published by the store manager at least **1 week in advance**.
- Shift swaps must be approved by the store manager.
- Remote work is not available for store and warehouse roles.

## Overtime
Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager and is paid according to Indonesian labour law.
Head office staff in grade G5 and above are not eligible for overtime pay.
