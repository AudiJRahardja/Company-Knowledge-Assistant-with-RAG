# New Employee Onboarding

## Before your first day
HR sends a welcome email about one week before you start, with your start time, location and documents to
bring: KTP, NPWP, BPJS cards (if you have them), bank account details, and a recent photo.

## Your first day
- **Head office**: arrive at 09:00 at reception (Jl. Wijaya Raya No. 88). HR will give you your badge, and IT
  will set up your laptop and accounts.
- **Stores and warehouses**: report to the store or warehouse manager at the start of your first shift.
- You will be introduced to your **onboarding buddy**, a colleague who helps you during your first 3 months.

## Mandatory training (first 2 weeks)
Complete these in the BrightLearn platform within **2 weeks** of starting:
1. Code of Conduct
2. Information Security Basics
3. Personal Data Protection (UU PDP)
4. Health and Safety (store and warehouse staff also do in-person safety training)

## 30-60-90 day plan
- **Day 30**: check-in with your manager about your role and goals.
- **Day 60**: feedback conversation with your manager and buddy.
- **Day 90**: probation review. Your manager confirms whether you pass probation.

## Useful first steps
- Fill in your profile, bank account and family details in BrightHub.
- Register your family for health insurance within 30 days.
- Set up MFA for your accounts.
- Join your team's chat channels and the company-wide #announcements channel.
