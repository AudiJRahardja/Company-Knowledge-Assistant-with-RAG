# Parental Leave Policy

## Maternity leave
- **3 months (about 13 weeks) of fully paid maternity leave**, usually taken as 1.5 months before and 1.5
  months after the birth. You can move more of it to after the birth with a doctor's approval.
- If there are medical complications for the mother or baby, maternity leave can be extended by up to **3 more
  months**, as allowed under the Mother and Child Welfare Law (UU KIA). Pay during the extension follows that law.
- In case of miscarriage: 1.5 months of paid leave, or as stated in the doctor's note.

## Paternity leave
Brightnest gives **10 working days of paid paternity leave**, more than the legal minimum. It can be taken in up
to two blocks within **60 days** of the birth.

## Adoption leave
Employees who adopt a child under 5 years old get **10 working days of paid leave**, taken within 60 days of
the adoption.

## Returning to work
- Nursing mothers can take paid breaks to express milk. Head office has a lactation room on the 3rd floor.
- After parental leave, you return to the same or an equivalent role.

## How to apply
Tell your manager and HR at least **1 month before** your planned leave start date, and submit the request in
BrightHub > Leave > Parental Leave with your doctor's estimate (HPL) or adoption documents.
