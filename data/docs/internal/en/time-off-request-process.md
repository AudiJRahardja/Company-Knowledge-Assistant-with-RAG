# How to Request Time Off

## Steps in BrightHub
1. Log in to BrightHub and go to **Leave > New Request**.
2. Choose the leave type: Annual, Sick, Family Care, Parental, or Special Leave.
3. Pick the dates (full or half day) and add a short note if needed.
4. Attach documents if required (for example a doctor's note).
5. Submit. Your manager gets a notification.

## Approval
- Your manager should approve or reject within **2 working days**. If they do not respond, the request is
  escalated to their manager automatically.
- You get an email when the request is approved. Do not book non-refundable travel before approval.
- Approved leave appears in your Google Calendar and your team's shared leave calendar.

## Cancelling or changing leave
Cancel or change a request in BrightHub > Leave > My Requests. If the leave has already started, ask your
manager to cancel the remaining days.

## Emergencies
If something urgent happens and you cannot request in advance, call or message your manager as soon as you
can, then submit the request in BrightHub within **2 working days** after you return.

## Special leave (paid, per Indonesian labour law)
| Event | Paid days |
|---|---|
| Your own wedding | 3 days |
| Your child's wedding | 2 days |
| Child's circumcision or baptism | 2 days |
| Death of spouse, child, parent or parent-in-law | 2 days |
| Death of another household member | 1 day |
| Hajj or Umrah (once during employment) | Up to 40 days, apply 3 months ahead |

## Questions
For problems with BrightHub, contact IT (ServiceDesk). For questions about your balance, contact HR.
