# Employee Handbook Overview

## Welcome
This handbook summarises how we work at Brightnest. Detailed policies (leave, working hours, reimbursements,
benefits, IT and so on) each have their own page. If a detailed policy and this overview disagree, the
detailed policy wins.

## Employment basics
- **Probation**: 3 months for permanent employees (PKWTT). Your manager does a probation review in your third
  month.
- **Contract staff (PKWT)**: the same policies apply unless your contract says otherwise.
- **Payday**: salaries are paid on the **25th of each month**. If the 25th is a weekend or public holiday,
  salary is paid on the last business day before it.
- **Payslips**: available in BrightHub (our HR portal) under My Pay.
- **THR** (religious holiday allowance): paid at least 7 days before Idul Fitri. See the Benefits page.

## Tools you will use
- **BrightHub**: HR portal for leave requests, payslips, expense claims and your personal details.
- **Google Workspace**: email, calendar and documents.
- **ServiceDesk**: IT help requests (see IT Help and Support).

## Updating your details
Keep your address, bank account, emergency contact and family details (for insurance) up to date in
BrightHub > My Profile. Changes to your bank account take effect from the next payroll if made before the
15th of the month.

## Where to get help
Start with your manager. For HR questions, contact your HR business partner or hr@brightnest.co.id. See
"Internal Contacts Directory" for names and extensions.
