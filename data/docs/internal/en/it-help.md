# IT Help and Support

## How to get IT help
- **ServiceDesk portal**: servicedesk.brightnest.co.id (fastest; you can track your ticket)
- **Email**: it-help@brightnest.co.id
- **Phone**: extension **2000** from the office, or +62 21 5000 2000
- **Hours**: Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 WIB
- **Store POS emergencies** (tills not working) outside office hours: on-call line **+62 811-0000-9999**, every
  day 08:00–22:00

## Response times
| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Store POS down, security incident | 30 minutes |
| High | Cannot work at all (laptop dead, no login) | 4 business hours |
| Normal | Software request, printer issue | 1 business day |

## Passwords and accounts
- Reset your password yourself at **password.brightnest.co.id** (you need your phone for verification).
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters. You are asked to change them every 180 days.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is required for email, BrightHub and VPN.
- IT will **never** ask for your password. Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button.

## Laptops and devices
- Head office staff get a company laptop. Do not install unapproved software; request it through ServiceDesk.
- Use the **company VPN** (GlobalConnect) when working outside the office.
- **Lost or stolen device**: report to IT within **24 hours** so we can lock it remotely.
- Laptops are replaced every 4 years.

## New joiners and leavers
Accounts for new employees are created by IT after HR confirms the start date. On your last day, return your
laptop and badge to IT.
