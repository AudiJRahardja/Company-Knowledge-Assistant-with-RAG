# Salary Bands 2026

**Confidential: HR only.** Do not share with employees or managers outside the approved compensation process.

## Monthly base salary bands (gross, IDR)
| Grade | Example roles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Sales associate, cashier, warehouse staff | 5,400,000 | 6,000,000 | 6,800,000 |
| G2 | Senior sales associate, driver, CS agent | 6,200,000 | 7,200,000 | 8,400,000 |
| G3 | Team leader, specialist, junior analyst | 8,000,000 | 9,800,000 | 11,500,000 |
| G4 | Assistant store manager, analyst, engineer | 11,000,000 | 14,000,000 | 17,000,000 |
| G5 | Store manager, senior engineer, lead | 16,000,000 | 21,000,000 | 26,000,000 |
| G6 | Department manager | 24,000,000 | 32,000,000 | 40,000,000 |
| G7 | Head of department | 38,000,000 | 50,000,000 | 65,000,000 |

## Regional adjustment
Store and warehouse grades G1–G2 follow the provincial minimum wage (UMP/UMK). Bandung and Surabaya bands for
G1–G2 are set at **95%** of the Jakarta band, but never below the local minimum wage.

## Rules
- New hires are normally offered between the minimum and the midpoint. Offers above the midpoint need approval
  from the Head of People.
- The yearly salary review takes place in March, with increases based on performance rating and position in
  band.
- Promotions to a higher grade come with at least a 10% increase or the new band minimum, whichever is higher.
