# Manager Guidelines

**For people managers and HR only.**

## Approving leave
- Respond to leave requests within **2 working days**. Unanswered requests escalate to your own manager.
- Do not reject annual leave without a business reason. If you reject, suggest alternative dates.
- During store peak periods (2 weeks before Idul Fitri, 15–31 December), area managers decide exceptions.

## Sick leave patterns
If a team member has frequent short sick leaves (for example, more than 4 separate absences in 3 months), have
a private, supportive conversation first. Involve your HR business partner before any formal step. Never ask
about specific medical details.

## Performance reviews
- **Mid-year check-in**: July. **Year-end review**: December.
- Ratings: 1 (Below expectations) to 5 (Exceptional). Ratings are calibrated with peers and HR before they are
  shared with employees.
- Target distribution guide: about 10% rated 5, 20% rated 4, 55% rated 3, 15% rated 1–2.

## Performance improvement plans (PIP)
Use a PIP when performance stays below expectations after documented feedback. A PIP lasts 60 days, with clear
goals and check-ins every 2 weeks. Always prepare a PIP together with your HR business partner. A PIP is not
started during an employee's parental or long sick leave.

## Hiring
- Open a hiring request in BrightHub > Recruitment. New positions need department head and Finance approval;
  replacements need department head approval only.
- Salary offers must stay within the band given by HR. You will not see full salary bands; HR provides the
  range for each offer.

## Confidentiality
Information about team members' performance, health, pay and personal matters must be kept confidential and
shared only with HR and your own manager.
